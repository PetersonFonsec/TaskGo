# ADR-001: Centralized Provider Profile Completion Journey

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-23

## Context

TaskGo providers need valid financial information before they can receive payments, but the current provider home does not explain payout readiness or guide providers through missing information. Profile information that improves trust and marketplace quality, such as a photo, social links, and addresses, is also distributed across incomplete or separate journeys.

The product must distinguish payment-blocking requirements from profile enrichment, serve both new and existing providers, and keep outstanding actions visible without implying that every profile field blocks payment.

## Decision

TaskGo will add a prominent provider-home CTA that opens a centralized profile completion journey with two separate checklists:

1. **Required to receive payments**: Pagar.me-confirmed bank account.
2. **Strengthen your profile**: photo, social links, and addresses.

Each section will communicate its own progress and remaining items. Financial requirements will receive the highest priority. The CTA will remain visible while either section has outstanding items and will apply to all providers with incomplete information.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: CTA With Links to Existing Screens

- **Description**: List incomplete items in the home card and direct each item to its existing destination.
- **Pros**: Reuses more of the current navigation and limits the initial product scope.
- **Cons**: Creates a fragmented journey and weakens the provider's understanding of overall progress.
- **Why rejected**: It does not provide the coherent completion experience needed to distinguish payout readiness from profile enrichment.

### Alternative 2: Two-Phase Financial-First Experience

- **Description**: Launch only bank-account completion in the MVP, then add photo, social links, and addresses in a later phase.
- **Pros**: Focuses initial delivery entirely on the primary payment-readiness outcome.
- **Cons**: Leaves the requested profile completion experience unfinished and postpones trust-building improvements.
- **Why rejected**: The selected centralized checklist can preserve financial priority while presenting all known profile actions clearly.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Providers can see exactly what prevents them from receiving payments.
- Optional profile improvements are visible without being misrepresented as payout blockers.
- New and existing providers receive the same guidance based on their current state.
- Separate progress indicators make the next action and overall completion status clear.

### Negative

- The product must align several currently separate profile activities into one consistent journey.
- A persistent CTA may compete with other provider-home content until all profile items are complete.

### Risks

- Providers may still confuse recommended information with mandatory requirements; distinct labels and visual hierarchy must reinforce the difference.
- A long checklist may reduce engagement; financial actions must stay prioritized and each step should remain concise.
- Customer Pix payment support may be confused with provider settlement; product messaging must state that provider payouts use the confirmed bank account.

## Implementation Notes

Implementation details will be defined in the subsequent technical specification. The product experience must preserve the distinction between payout eligibility and profile enrichment.
